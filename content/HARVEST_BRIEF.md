## Explicit avoid list

- **Non-English** titles/hooks/transcripts (Spanish/Portuguese/Arabic/etc.)
- **Hiring / job-ad** posts (diesel fleet hiring, CDL wanted, Uber/Lyft driver recruiting) with no tip/hook craft
- **Empty or emoji-only** X/IG posts; blank titles; “post” placeholders
- **CTA comment spam**: “Comment X and I’ll send…”, “Follow and comment PFP for the prompt”, pinned-comment bait
- **Duplicate ChatGPT tip listicles**: “N ways/hacks/tips to use ChatGPT”, “feels illegal/cheating/unfair” clones, “laziest way to make money with AI” repeats
- **Near-duplicate URLs/titles** in-category (keep highest views only)
- **Face-on true_crime personality vlogs** with weak packaging (storytime/reaction); faceless first
- **Rideshare clone tutorials**: repeated “how to accept rides” / “N tips for new Uber drivers” once 1–2 exemplars exist
- **Clickfarm / no craft signal** even at high views

## Quality bar (reminder)

Keep only cards that teach transferable packaging: strong title/hook, clear `pattern_tags`, usable `transcript_30s` or hooks, English, real thumb when possible.

Weak-keep OK: low views but clear craft; thin transcript but strong title+hook; missing thumb but otherwise strong.
