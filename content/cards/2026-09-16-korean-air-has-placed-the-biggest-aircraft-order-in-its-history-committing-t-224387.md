## Transcript (~30s)

Korean Air has placed the biggest aircraft order in its history, committing to 103 Boeing jets, but there’s a pretty extraordinary detail hiding inside the shopping list: 78 of them are versions that still aren’t certified for commercial service.

The record order includes 20 Boeing 777-9s, 25 787-10s, 50 737-10s and eight 777-8 Freighters. Of those four variants, only the 787-10 is currently certified and carrying passengers. Boeing’s long-delayed 777-9 and 737-10 are still awaiting certification, while the 777-8F remains in development.

It’s an enormous vote of confidence in aircraft Boeing hasn’t finished bringing to market yet.

The jets carry a combined $36.2 billion list-price value, although IBA estimates their current market value after typical discounts at around $12.6 billion. Korean Air has also finalized roughly $8.6 billion in spare-engine purchases and maintenance agreements as it prepares for its integration with Asiana.

Basically, Korean Air has just gone shopping for its future fleet and three quarters of the order isn’t allowed to enter airline service yet. #KoreanAir #Boeing #Aviation
