## Transcript (~30s)

RaaS is harder to execute than the pitch deck suggests.

The recurring revenue story is clean. The operational reality is not. Uptime guarantees, field service coverage, SLA disputes, fleet maintenance costs — these eat margin fast if you haven't modeled them carefully.

Locus Robotics, Roboworx, and Aescape are putting people on a panel at RoboBusiness 2026 (Oct. 20, Santa Clara) to get into the actual mechanics: contract structure, customer success metrics, unit economics at scale.

For anyone pricing a RaaS offering right now: how are you handling the gap between promised uptime and the real cost of delivering it in the field?
