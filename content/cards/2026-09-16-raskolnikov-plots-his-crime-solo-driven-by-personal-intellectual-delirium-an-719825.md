## Transcript (~30s)

Raskolnikov plots his crime solo, driven by personal intellectual delirium, and messes it up by unexpectedly having to kill an innocent bystander (Lizaveta). Conversely, the real-world crime was a calculated, outsourced conspiracy. While Raskolnikov acted alone out of ideological mania, the Obado case is a cold example of a modern, institutionalized abuse of power.
