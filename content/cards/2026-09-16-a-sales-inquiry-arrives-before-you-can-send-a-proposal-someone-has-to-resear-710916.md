## Transcript (~30s)

A sales inquiry arrives. Before you can send a proposal, someone has to research the buyer, check fit, define the scope, and verify the price.

That’s where AI can help - but each step needs a clear job:

→ Research: gather facts and sources; flag what’s unknown.
→ Qualify: check the lead against your business criteria.
→ Draft: use approved scope, claims, and pricing.
→ Verify: check the evidence, requirements, and totals.
→ Human approval: confirm the exact proposal and recipients.
→ Send + log: record the result in your CRM and check before retrying.

The useful measure isn’t how many proposals AI can write. It’s whether you send accurate proposals faster, with less rework.

So when is a simple automation enough - and when are AI agents worth the extra cost?

My guide explores 13 business workflows, 12 tools, and 3 practical agent teams, with an illustrative cost model to help you assess whether a project could pay off.
