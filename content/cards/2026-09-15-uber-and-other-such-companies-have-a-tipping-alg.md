## Transcript (~30s)

Uber and other such companies have a “tipping algorithm” that manipulates the base pay of drivers.

So, when you tip a driver on the app, it matches the tip with a lower base wage, making the generosity pointless by giving the extra money to the company.

Always tip in cash.
