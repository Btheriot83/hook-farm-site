## Transcript (~30s)

Some cities without Guard deployments have also recorded spectacular declines. For example, first-half homicide declines of 61% in Little Rock and 76% in Rochester. Chicago finished 2025 with homicides down about 30%, New York murders were down about 20%, and Los Angeles recorded its fewest homicides since 1966—all without Guard deployments.
Also, Memphis's crime collapse began well before the National Guard arrived. Trump created the Memphis Safe Task Force on September 15, 2025. Task-force operations began around September 29, and Tennessee National Guard patrols didn't begin until October 10. By then:
Overall crime had already fallen 13% in 2024. Through September 2025—essentially before the Guard was patrolling—Part I crime was already down 23%. Homicides were already down 22% through September 2025.
And the broader data show that Memphis crime peaked around 2023 and had been falling substantially thereafter. (Per ChatGPT edited)
