## Transcript (~30s)

Close to 300 workers employed by China Railway Seventh Group have downed tools following the death of a colleague in a workplace accident on Monday.

Thirty-five-year-old diesel mechanic Lazarus Hedimbi died after a pressure container exploded and injured him in the chest.

The workers stopped working because, according to employees, management refused their request for time off to mourn their colleague.

Minister of Works and Transport Veikko Nekundi has since arrived at the company’s offices, where a meeting with management is underway.

Some workers who spoke to NBC News raised concerns about workplace safety and claimed that they are not provided with adequate protective equipment or safety measures.

One worker, who was wearing a cast on his arm after sustaining a workplace injury, claimed he was also denied time off to recover and deal with the injury.

The workers are constructing the Western Bypass highway behind UNAM.
