## Transcript (~30s)

Washington just killed the AI slowdown while cheap Chinese models quietly took over the token economy.

- Trump called AI-takeover talk a "hoax," then phoned Jensen Huang live at the All-In Summit to say slowing America only helps China. Any US pause is off the table. Build as if compute, open weights, and Chinese labs keep accelerating.

- Chinese models now hold roughly 72% of OpenRouter's top-ten tokens, and GLM 5.3 Flash is Claude Code's most-used model at 4.4x Opus volume. Agents optimize dollars per token, not brand. If you default every workload to frontier APIs, you are overpaying.

- Salesforce shipped Koa, a CRM reasoner post-trained in-house from Nvidia's open Nemotron model on fully synthetic data. It matched or beat frontier models with 3x fewer errors. The enterprise pattern is now: own the high-volume reasoning layer, rent frontier for the edge cases.

- A ChatGPT co-inventor launched Jev, a non-generative model that "can't hallucinate" because it only picks among preset options. Claimed cost: about 238x below frontier pricing. Stop paying frontier prices for tasks that are really multiple-choice.

- Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI are discussing a FINRA-style testing body, but nobody agrees on enforcement, and Beijing calls the whole safety push a cartel move. Your own evals, logging, and kill-switches are the only controls you will actually have.

The signal: prestige models are losing share to price, domain fit, and in-house post-training, while US AI policy becomes a partisan fight instead of a technical one. Route by task, not by brand. #AI #ArtificialIntelligence #AIPolicy #EnterpriseAI #OpenWeights
