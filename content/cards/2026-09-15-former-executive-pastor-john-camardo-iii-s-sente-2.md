Former Executive Pastor John Camardo III’s Sentencing Adjourned

John Camardo III, the former Executive Pastor of The Chapel in Western New York, was scheduled to be sentenced on September 10 after pleading guilty to second-degree rape and second-degree course of sexual conduct against a child in connection with the sexual abuse of two minor victims.

That sentencing did not happen.

After receiving numerous questions about the status of the case, I contacted the Erie County District Attorney’s Office directly. The DA’s office confirmed to me that Camardo’s sentencing was adjourned at the request of defense counsel.

His new sentencing date is October 19, 2026, at 11:00 a.m.

I have since received additional information from a credible source with knowledge of the case regarding what may be happening behind the scenes.

According to my source, Camardo’s defense is attempting to have his sentence reduced, and his attorney is having him undergo two psychosexual evaluations, reportedly in an effort to present evidence that he does not pose a significant risk.

When Camardo pleaded guilty, the Erie County District Attorney’s Office announced that he faced a determinate sentence of 6–8 years in state prison.

I want to make an important distinction: the DA’s office independently confirmed that the adjournment was requested by the defense. The information regarding the psychosexual evaluations and an effort to reduce Camardo’s sentence comes from a credible source and has not yet been independently verified through court records.

I am actively working to obtain any available records surrounding the defense’s request for the adjournment. I am also investigating whether members of The Chapel or others associated with the church have submitted character letters on Camardo’s behalf ahead of sentencing. I have not confirmed that such letters exist at this time.

This case is far from over. I will continue investigating and will provide updates as additional information can be independently confirmed.
