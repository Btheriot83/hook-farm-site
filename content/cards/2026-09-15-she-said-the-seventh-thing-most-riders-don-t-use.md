## Transcript (~30s)

She said the seventh thing most riders don't use is the "Wait and Save" option — and it's the easiest discount on the platform.

Both Uber and Lyft offer a ride option — often called "UberX Saver" or Lyft's equivalent — that gives you a lower fare in exchange for a longer pickup window. Instead of a driver arriving in 3–5 minutes, you agree to wait 10–20 minutes.

"UberX Saver is typically 15–30% cheaper than standard UberX. Same car. Same driver quality. Same route. Same destination. The only difference is you wait a few extra minutes for the driver to arrive. The app uses the extra time to find a driver who's already heading in your direction — which is more efficient for the driver and cheaper for you."

She said most riders never select this option because it's not the default.

"The app defaults to the standard UberX or Lyft option. Wait and Save is listed below it — sometimes you have to scroll down to see it. Most riders tap the first option and confirm without looking at alternatives. They don't realize that 2 inches of scrolling and 10 minutes of patience would save them $4–$8 per ride."

She said she uses Wait and Save for any ride where she's not in a rush.

"Going to the grocery store? Wait and Save. Heading to a friend's house on a Saturday afternoon? Wait and Save. Morning commute where I leave 15 minutes earlier than usual? Wait and Save. The only times I use standard pricing are when I'm late for something or when surge makes even the Saver option expensive. For every casual ride, the extra wait is worth the savings."
