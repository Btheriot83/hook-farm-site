## Transcript (~30s)

Asian trucks are certainly winning fleets, and affordability is a big reason why. The price advantage compared to European trucks is difficult to ignore.

That advantage, however, does not mean maintenance needs to follow the same price philosophy. There is always a compromise between price and quality, and using high quality OEM parts can help these trucks remain reliable and last much longer.

A more affordable truck maintained with quality parts makes perfect sense. A win win for the fleet owner.
