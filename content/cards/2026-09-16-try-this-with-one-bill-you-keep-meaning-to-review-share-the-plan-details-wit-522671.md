Try this with one bill you keep meaning to review: share the plan details with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another assistant, after removing account numbers, addresses, and payment information.

1/ Ask: “Summarize what I pay, when the plan renews, how cancellation works, and which charges are optional. Then suggest questions I should ask the provider about cheaper equivalent plans.”

3/ Before changing anything, confirm the price, fees, contract length, and cancellation terms directly with the provider. AI is useful here as a document reader and conversation coach, not as the final authority.
