## Transcript (~30s)

She said the fourth thing most riders don't realize is that their rating affects everything — the quality of drivers they're matched with, how quickly they get picked up, and whether drivers cancel on them.

Every Uber and Lyft rider has a rating between 1.0 and 5.0. Most riders have never checked theirs. She said it's one of the most important numbers in the app — and most riders are sabotaging it without knowing.

"When a ride request comes in, the driver sees your rating before they decide whether to accept. A rider with a 4.9 rating gets accepted instantly. A rider with a 4.6 rating gets cancelled on. A rider with a 4.5 or below has drivers actively avoiding them. This happens in real time, every single ride. The lower your rating, the longer your wait time, the worse the drivers who eventually accept, and the higher your effective cost — because by the time a driver finally accepts, the surge may have shifted."

She said most riders don't know what hurts their rating.

"The things that tank your rating as a rider: slamming the car door, eating in the car, being rude or dismissive, making the driver wait more than 2 minutes at pickup, giving the driver wrong directions, leaving trash in the backseat, not being at the pickup point when the driver arrives, requesting stops that take 10+ minutes, and — the biggest one — being ready to go when the car arrives versus making the driver circle the block 3 times."

She said you can check your rating in the Uber app by tapping your profile icon — it's displayed right under your name. On Lyft, go to the menu and tap your profile picture.

"If your rating is below 4.7, you're losing matches. If it's below 4.6, top-rated drivers are actively declining your requests. The fix is simple: be outside and visible when the car arrives, say hello, don't slam the door, don't leave garbage, and tip. That's it. Do those 5 things consistently and your rating will climb above 4.85 within a few weeks. The difference in driver quality and pickup speed between a 4.65 and a 4.9 is massive."
