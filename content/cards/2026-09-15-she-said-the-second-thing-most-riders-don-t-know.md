## Transcript (~30s)

She said the second thing most riders don't know is that walking 2–3 blocks before requesting a ride can drop the price by $5–$15 — because surge pricing is zone-based, not city-wide.

This is the trick she said saves her the most money on nights out.

"Surge pricing isn't applied to the entire city at once. It's applied by geographic zones — small hexagonal areas the algorithm uses to calculate local supply and demand. When you're standing outside a concert venue, a stadium, or a busy bar district at 11:30 PM, your zone has 500 people requesting rides and 12 available drivers. The surge multiplier spikes to 2x or 3x."

She said the zone next to you — literally 2–3 blocks away — often has a completely different multiplier.

"Walk 200–400 meters away from the venue or the bar strip. Open the app again. The fare often drops significantly because you've crossed into an adjacent zone with lower demand. You're still going to the same destination. The driver still picks you up. The ride still takes the same amount of time. But the starting fare is $8–$15 less because you requested from a zone the algorithm isn't surging."

She said this works because the surge calculation happens at the moment of request, based on your GPS location.

"The algorithm checks: where is the rider right now? What's the demand in this zone? What's the supply? It calculates the multiplier and shows you the fare. If you move 2 blocks and re-check, the algorithm recalculates based on the new zone. The demand at your new location is lower. The fare drops. It's the same ride. You just changed where you pressed the button."

She said she does this every single time she leaves a crowded area.

"After a concert, I never request from the venue. I walk 3 blocks toward a quieter street, then request. After a night out in a bar district, I walk to the edge of the neighborhood before opening the app. Three minutes of walking saves me $8–$15 every time. Over a year of going out twice a week, that's $800–$1,500 in savings from a 3-minute walk."
