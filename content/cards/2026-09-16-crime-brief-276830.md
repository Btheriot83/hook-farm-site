## Transcript (~30s)

CRIME BRIEF
Witness intimidation cited in unsolved-homicide research
PLACE: United States (multiple cities)
FACT (2015-2023): Criminologists and police officials in several high-violence cities have cited witness intimidation and community distrust as contributing factors in declining homicide clearance rates.
NATIONAL CLAIM: Clearance rates falling is purely a resourcing problem, nothing to do with witnesses
LESSON:
SOURCE: Murder Accountability Project clearance-rate research
While some argue that falling homicide clearance rates are strictly a byproduct of insufficient police resources, research from the Murder Accountability Project suggests a more complex reality. Criminologists and law enforcement officials in several high-violence cities have identified witness intimidation and deep-seated community distrust as specific, documented obstacles to solving crimes. These findings indicate that systemic barriers to cooperation, rather than just staffing shortages, significantly impact the ability to close homicide cases.
