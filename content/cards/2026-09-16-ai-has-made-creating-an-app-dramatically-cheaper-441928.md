## Transcript (~30s)

AI has made creating an app dramatically cheaper.

You can describe a product to Claude, Codex, or Cursor and have a working prototype before lunch.

BUT turning that prototype into a sovereign onchain product is still a completely different job.

You need consensus, validators, wallets, an explorer, security, liquidity, token infrastructure, and someone who knows how to connect everything without setting the building on fire.

That gap is where @CNPYNetwork fits.

Backed by @Arrington_Cap , @borderless_cap , @fenbushi , @snzholding , and others, Canopy is trying to become the singular stack for building an onchain app and launching it in one go.

Builders start with open-source templates, write in familiar languages, and use the AI coding tools they already know.

Instead of deploying another smart contract onto someone else’s blockspace, the product launches as a Nested Chain: an application-specific chain with its own logic, economics, and upgrade path.

Canopy then provides the validator network, shared security, wallet, explorer, and launch infrastructure. A new chain can borrow security from validators already bonding $CNPY rather than convincing an independent validator set to appear on day one.

If the app grows, it is not trapped forever.

It can remain under Canopy’s shared-security system or progressively move toward independent L1 operation.

Terminal adds the economic layer.

Creators can launch app tokens on a bonding curve, earn trading fees, then “graduate” into DEX liquidity and staking—while keeping chain ownership.

What each group gets:

- Builders: familiar languages, AI tools, no Solidity-first overhead, no DevOps/validator scramble
- Creators: bonding-curve launches, creator fees, DEX graduation, chain ownership
- Users: built-in wallet + explorer, token trading, possible app-token + CNPY staking rewards
- Stakers: one CNPY position can secure multiple Nested Chains and earn root + chain incentives
- Agents: APIs/templates designed for code-generating agents

➥ Why $CNPY matters

CNPY powers fees, secures validators, can be restaked across committees, and anchors Terminal launches + graduations.

Demand loop
- Terminal: launches/graduations drive CNPY velocity and staking after graduation
- Security: more chains → more committees → more CNPY bonded
- Usage: fees paid in CNPY recycle into rewards
- Builder flywheel: more apps → better examples for agents → faster launches

CEX/DEX listings help distribution, but access alone isn’t demand.

Canopy’s most interesting potential moat, however, may be its open-source flywheel. Every public application could expand a library of working examples for future coding agents.

--

➥ Final Thesis

More apps → smarter agents → shorter build cycles → even more apps and CNPY adoption.

That’s the core Canopy thesis, and it’s what ends up expressed in CNPY.

Not a ghost chain hoping developers show up someday, but an AI-first assembly line where the concept, the app, the chain, the security, and the token launch ship in one unbroken pipeline.

Now it comes down to this: can Canopy convert the hype into products people actually stick with?
