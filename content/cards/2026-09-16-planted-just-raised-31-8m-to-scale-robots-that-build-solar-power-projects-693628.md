## Transcript (~30s)

Planted just raised $31.8M to scale robots that build solar power projects.

The headline is not “a robot installs solar.”

The real story: power deployment is becoming a manufacturing problem.

Planted says it completed field construction on a 28 MW behind-the-meter project in under 3 months, and is targeting 100 MW deployed in 2026 after more than 10 MW in 2025.

If this model works at scale, the winners won’t just be robotics companies.

They’ll include:

- Project-finance lenders
- Robot maintenance and fleet-operations providers
- Battery, spares, and remote-monitoring suppliers
- EPC and grid-interconnection partners
- Insurers learning to underwrite robotic construction
- Data-center developers buying “powered land”

The robot is only the visible layer.

The economic shift is that electricity capacity could become a manufactured output—not a one-off construction project.

What to watch: can robot-built power projects become reliably financeable at hundreds-of-megawatts scale?
