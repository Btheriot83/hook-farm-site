🔴 Navy vet's 1987 murder solved after suspect provides DNA, then dies by suicide

Orison James Chafin, 54, a Korean War Navy veteran, was found dead in a Glendale, Arizona cemetery on August 8, 1987, with his throat cut. The case went unsolved for nearly 39 years until the investigation was reopened in 2025.
Gary Wayne Billups emerged as the suspect. Detectives had recovered three male DNA profiles from evidence at the scene—including material from beer bottles and cigarette butts—but found no matches until recently.
