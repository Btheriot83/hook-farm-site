## Transcript (~30s)

Most drivers still think they are “independent” on Uber.
But if you've driven recently, you'll know the truth:
Accept almost every job = constant pings and around £20 an hour.
Start rejecting the bad jobs = suddenly the app goes quiet.
Long waits. Terrible trips. Fewer offers.
Uber will never admit it...
But it feels like the algorithm rewards drivers who accept everything, and quietly punishes drivers who don't.
You still have an accept button and a reject button.
But is it really a choice if one gets rewarded... and the other gets punished?
The freedom is still there on paper.
But the algorithm controls what happens next.
Watch until the end if you've ever felt “shadow banned.” 👀
