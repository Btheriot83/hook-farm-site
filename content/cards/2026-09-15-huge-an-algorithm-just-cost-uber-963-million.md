## Transcript (~30s)

HUGE: An algorithm just cost Uber $963 MILLION.

Dutch regulators fined Uber for letting automated systems suspend driver accounts without human review between 2020 and 2022.

Drivers suspected of fraud or with low ratings were cut off from their livelihood by a computer, per Reuters.

It is the second-largest GDPR fine ever, behind only Meta's $1.2 BILLION penalty in 2023.

Uber says it will appeal.
