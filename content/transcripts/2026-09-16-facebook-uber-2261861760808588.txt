Is the Dutch reach? It's not the latest dance move. The Dutch reach can be a safer way to open a car door. Adopting this method can help keep people on bikes safer. Step one, with the opposite hand, reach towards the door. Step 2, twist your body towards the window. Step three, look over your shoulder for bikes or scooters. Step four, when clear, open slowly and exit. Maybe the Dutch reach can be the latest dance move.
