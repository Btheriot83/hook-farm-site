The Susquehanna Hose Company is proud to place Quint 55 in service at Division 5!

This new @EONE_FireTrucks Cyclone HR100 Aerial Ladder is powered by a 525 HP Cummins X12 engine and features a 100-foot heavy-duty aerial ladder with an electric pre-piped master stream waterway, a 1,500 GPM Hale QMAX midship pump, 480-gallon booster tank, 20 gallons of Class A foam, Onspot automatic tire chains, and a full complement of battery-powered HURST Rescue Tools.

Getting Quint 55 into service has been a tremendous team effort. 17 drivers have been cleared after completing 16 hours of training each, while an additional 13 non-drivers completed a six-hour skills checklist.

In total, our members logged more than 350 hours of training and an additional 150 hours of preparation work to meet our September 15 target date.

A massive thank you to Committee Chairman Sam Cubberley and committee members Neal Campbell, Steven Buchanan, Taylor Miller, James Miller, Craig Triplett, Don Graybeal, Chad Myers, Brian Remines, and Alex Cardwell for their countless hours of work helping bring this project to completion.

And to all of our members who put in the time to train, prepare, and get Quint 55 ready to respond — excellent work!

Quint 55 represents another significant investment in the equipment, training, and capabilities of the Susquehanna Hose Company and the communities we serve.
