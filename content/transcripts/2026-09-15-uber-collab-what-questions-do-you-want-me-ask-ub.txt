Uber Collab — What questions do you want me ask Uber execs?

Uber is paying me to attend their driver meetup in Miami next week.

At first, it felt awkward, almost like sleeping with the enemy kinda thing. But that’s a foolish mindset, because if Uber was the “enemy” I shouldn’t be driving with them. So I’m going. Uber said I get creative freedom and they want my honest opinion. I wouldn’t go if they wanted me to be an Uber spokesperson. 

I’d like to ask Dara, “how would you define the perfect driver?” I’m not sure if I’ll even get a chance to talk with him.

Obviously, I’m going to learn what new things Uber has in store for drivers. I assume some are good and others don’t really matter. I don’t know what to expect.

But mostly I’m hoping to connect with other drivers and people at Uber. Hopefully make things better for drivers. ❤️

Let me know what questions I can as Uber while I’m there. 

We’ll see…
