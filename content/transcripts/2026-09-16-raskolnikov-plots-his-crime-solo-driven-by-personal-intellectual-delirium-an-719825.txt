Raskolnikov plots his crime solo, driven by personal intellectual delirium, and messes it up by unexpectedly having to kill an innocent bystander (Lizaveta). Conversely, the real-world crime was a calculated, outsourced conspiracy. The High Court found that Obado financed and instigated the plot, while his aides handled the physical execution and cover-up. While Raskolnikov acted alone out of ideological mania, the Obado case is a cold example of a modern, institutionalized abuse of power.
