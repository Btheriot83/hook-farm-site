WHAT IT IS
- Tesla’s paid autonomous ride service. You book a car in the Robotaxi app. The car drives. There is no human driver on unsupervised rides.
- Live cars today are mostly Model Y. The purpose-built Cybercab is the volume vehicle testers say Tesla is prioritizing.
- Camera-only vision. Tesla VP of AI Ashok Elluswamy: no lidar, radar, or HD maps required for safe operation on this stack.
- This how-to merges the public Robotaxi service folder with Owning Robotaxi Fleet (owner / private-fleet economics). Not Cybercab-only bookmarks.

HOW IT WORKS
- Download the Tesla Robotaxi app and join the waitlist. Official robotaxi (Sep 2025): app is available to all; access expands over time.
- Book the ride in Tesla’s app. Elon: Tesla will not partner with Uber or Lyft. “We will have enough demand.”
- Unsupervised means no safety driver. Supervised cities still have a person in the car.
- Official robotaxi (Aug 27): service hours are 6am to 10pm, 7 days/week. Unsupervised fleet is a lot bigger. Upgraded vehicle distribution and routing so you wait less.
- Testers: you can change destination and keep the same trip for multiple stops.
- Cars return to a depot or homebase and park themselves (Houston footage).
- Ashok: FSD v15 is already running on Model Y robotaxis and will run on Cybercab.
- Testers: Tesla is limiting Model Y conversions to prioritize Cybercab, built with an unboxed modular process.

OWNING / FLEET (from Owning Robotaxi Fleet dump)
- Elon (Feb 2026, also in Robotaxi folder): Tesla owners will be able to add their cars to the Robotaxi network; Tesla collects 25% of revenue.
- Fleet testers frame the network like Airbnb for cars: owner adds vehicle; Tesla handles software, routing, payments, rider experience; platform fee often modeled ~25–35%; owner keeps the rest after operating costs (tester).
- Fleet / Teslaconomics (Mar 2026): ~$30k/yr net profit per Robotaxi to owner is a base-case assumption from analyst models + Tesla long-term vision — after platform fee, charging, tires, maintenance, insurance, cleaning. No official real-world owner earnings in this dump. Profit/mile chatter ~$0.25–$0.50 after costs at 50k–100k+ miles/yr in high-demand areas.
- Fleet / Teslaconomics: Cybercab cost chatter ~$25k–$30k; Elon told that tester production was starting in ~1 month (Mar 2026 context — treat as dated tester claim). Rough capital for a ~$1M/yr fleet ~$850k–$1M in vehicles (tester arithmetic).
- Fleet / chamath: intends to buy and deploy large fleets nationally when possible; expects payback and positive FCF in under 2 years (tester / investor claim, not Official Tesla).
- Fleet / BrianRoemmele: “all in” on buying into the owning-fleet thesis now; separate Optimus ownership program mentioned but not published in this dump.
- Fleet / tslaming (Mar 2026): then only 8 unsupervised Austin robotaxis — historical; do not use as current fleet size (Robotaxi dump Aug 23 tester: 908 total).
- Testers / Teslaconomics (Aug 25, Robotaxi folder): “owning your own Tesla Robotaxi fleet can one day make you financially free.” Still a tester slogan — no Official owner-earnings number in this dump.
- CONFLICT / caution: owner earnings, Cybercab sticker, ROI, and payback are tester models. Dump explicitly says none of it is guaranteed. Do not treat as Official Tesla guidance.

SAFETY
- Elon: scale as fast as possible, but do not harm anyone. One injury becomes a worldwide headline and a regulatory clampdown.
- Ashok (Jul 2026): zero notable incidents over 380,000 unsupervised miles, across six cities in two states. Cameras only.
- Testers on NHTSA ADS data (Jun 16, 2025–Jul 15, 2026): zero collisions initiated by the Model Y ADS. Two new incidents were other drivers hitting a stopped or parked Robotaxi (Austin red-arrow stop; Dallas parked). No passengers, no injuries, no tow.
- Austin: testers said 100% of tracked robotaxi rides in that NHTSA window were unsupervised.
- Testers: 7x safer than the average human driver is an FSD claim in this folder, not a Robotaxi-only official stat.
