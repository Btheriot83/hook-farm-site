Derek Todd Lee, better known as the Baton Rouge Serial Killer, terrorized Louisiana in the late 1990s and early 2000s. He is officially linked to 7 murders, but investigators believe he may have killed even more women. For years, police struggled to identify him because another serial killer, Sean Vincent Gillis, was hunting women in Louisiana at the same time. Investigators were also thrown off when witnesses initially described Derek Todd Lee as a white man, delaying his capture. Lee targeted women around Baton Rouge and surrounding areas, stalking them before brutally attacking. DNA evidence eventually exposed the truth and led to his arrest in 2003.
