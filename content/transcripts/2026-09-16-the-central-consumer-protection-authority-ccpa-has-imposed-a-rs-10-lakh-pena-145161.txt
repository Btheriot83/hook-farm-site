The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) has imposed a Rs 10 lakh penalty on ride-hailing platform Rapido for alleged misleading advertisements, unfair trade practices, unfair contract terms and the use of deceptive “dark patterns”, the government said on Tuesday.

The action against Roppen Transportation Services Pvt Ltd, which operates Rapido, follows a sector-wide examination of cab and bike-taxi aggregators over practices including pre-ride tipping and dynamic pricing.

The CCPA said Rapido prompted riders to pay more while their bookings were still being processed. Messages displayed on the app included, “Higher the price, higher the chance of getting a ride” and suggestions to add Rs 10, Rs 20 or Rs 30 when drivers were not accepting the quoted fare.

According to the regulator, the app first quoted a fare and, after the booking was made, prompted users to increase the amount, creating an impression that paying more was necessary to secure the ride.

The CCPA classified the practice as “confirm shaming”, a dark pattern that creates urgency or fear of losing a service unless the consumer agrees to an additional payment.

The authority also flagged the colour-coded “Set your price” slider, saying it could influence users’ pricing decisions.
The CCPA noted that tips are ordinarily voluntary and should not be presented as a condition for receiving a service. It said Rapido’s submissions on tips were rejected.
