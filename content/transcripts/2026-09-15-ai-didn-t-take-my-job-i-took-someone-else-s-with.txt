AI didn’t take my job - I took someone else’s with AI.

A real story.

About 1.5 years ago, I was leading a team of 10 developers. We didn’t have a DevOps engineer, but we had to deploy a project with a complex infrastructure setup on GCP. Since we lacked the expertise, we hired a company to handle the deployment.

They assigned us a junior dev full-time and a tech lead at 50% capacity -someone with extensive GCP experience - tasked with automating everything using Terraform.

They charged us a lot of money. And delivered 💩.

I was shocked because the same company had done a solid job on a previous project. I had never used Terraform before, but even I could tell the code was bad.

Frustrated, I decided to take matters into my own hands.

I rewrote the entire Terraform codebase from scratch, leveraging ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot. But this wasn’t just vibe-coding -I was learning Terraform along the way, reading the docs, consulting ChatGPT, and making all the architectural decisions myself. By the end, I understood 100% of the code and infrastructure.

The result?
 ✅ The project launched successfully.
 ✅ We passed an external pentest and infrastructure audit.
 ✅ We achieved a perfect score on all ISO 27001 and SOC 2 requirements.
 ✅ The system has been running smoothly ever since.

And just like that, I became the DevOps person on our team—while still leading the team and building features. AI helped me learn Terraform so fast that we saved the cost of at least one full-time hire. Without AI, I wouldn’t have even attempted this.
