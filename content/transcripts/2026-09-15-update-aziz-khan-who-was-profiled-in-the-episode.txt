UPDATE: Aziz Khan, who was profiled in the episode ‘Abducted by a Parent’ on @netflix, has been found near Denver, Colorado. His father Abdul has met with the Douglas County Sheriff’s Office, and more information can be found on their website. http://bit.ly/4ir8HPv 

Aziz was abducted by his mother, Rabia Khalid, in 2017 and is now 14 years old. Both Rabia and her husband Elliot Bourgeois are currently in custody.
