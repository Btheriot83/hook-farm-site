Kourtney Nichole (@crimewithkourt) has built a global audience of more than 3 million followers through her focused, fact-driven true crime reporting.

Now, she’s bringing that investigative lens to The Final Hours, a new weekly @CrimeHouse original series powered by @PAVEStudios and co-hosted alongside Sarah Turney.

Together, they examine the final hours before someone goes missing, focusing on overlooked details, unanswered questions, and the moments that often matter most.
