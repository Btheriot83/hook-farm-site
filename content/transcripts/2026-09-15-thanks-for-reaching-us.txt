We understand the concern,the cars moving across your map before you book aren't actual vehicles waiting for your request—they are visual placeholders generated by an optimistic algorithm designed to show general supply in your area. When you finally hit Confirm, Uber broadcasts your ride request to nearby drivers one by one. If those drivers decline the trip because of low pay rates, an inconvenient destination, or an upcoming break, the algorithm expands its search radius until an available driver accepts—which is often someone 20 to 25 minutes away. To minimize these delays, you can request your ride 5 to 10 minutes early to absorb the dispatch buffer, opt for a higher-tier ride like Uber Comfort to boost driver acceptance rates, or use Uber Reserve for time-sensitive trips to secure a driver in advance.
