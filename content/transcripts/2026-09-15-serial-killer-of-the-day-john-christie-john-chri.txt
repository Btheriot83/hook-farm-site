Serial Killer of the Day: John Christie John Christie appeared to be an ordinary neighbor, but behind closed doors he was one of Britain's most disturbing killers. Between the 1940s and 1950s, he lured women to his home at 10 Rillington Place, where he strangled and murdered them. Christie even killed his own wife and hid multiple bodies inside the house.
