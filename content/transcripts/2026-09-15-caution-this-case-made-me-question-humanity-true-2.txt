Michael Cochran had a life, two children, and a wife he trusted. Then, on February 6, 2019, a flight to a Virginia bank was suddenly cancelled — the same day Michael collapsed at home.

Days later, he was dead.

What followed was a $2.5 million Ponzi scheme, insulin found in the house, an exhumed body, and one chilling question: why did a non-diabetic father have a blood sugar level of 21?

The danger wasn’t outside his life. Prosecutors said it was inside his marriage.
