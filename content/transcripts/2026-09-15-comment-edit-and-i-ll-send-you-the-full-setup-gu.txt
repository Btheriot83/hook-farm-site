ChatGPT can now edit videos for you.

Upload any video and it adds captions, cuts out dead space, and even adds full on screen animations.

Here's how to set it up in 30 seconds:

Go to ChatGPT → choose Codex (top left)
Click Plugins → search "Remotion" → Install Plugin
Drag and drop any video into Codex
Say: "Edit this video, add on screen captions, cut every silence, and animate the key moments on screen"

ChatGPT hands you back a perfectly edited video ready to post.
