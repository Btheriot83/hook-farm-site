5 - Uber / Lyft / Taxies

Same idea here - order an Uber from a location in your city driving to your GBP. 

Ask the driver to turn on google maps driving directions, and mention you’ll give a tip if they leave a review once there. 

Bonus: Ask them if they have any friends who would be interested in this and incentivise multiple referrals again. 

Role this out slowly, and try to aim for an extra 3 a week from this. Don’t overdo it.
