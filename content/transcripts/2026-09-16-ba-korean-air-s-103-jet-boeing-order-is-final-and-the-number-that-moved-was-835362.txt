$BA - KOREAN AIR'S 103-JET BOEING ORDER IS FINAL, AND THE NUMBER THAT MOVED WAS NOT THE AIRPLANES

Korean Air signed the definitive contracts in Seoul for 103 Boeing jets, 50 of them 737-10s, the fleet it wants once it has absorbed Asiana. Thirteen months ago this was a memorandum announced in Washington as a $50 billion purchase. It closed at $44.8 billion.

The airplanes did not move. They were $36.2 billion in the August 2025 memorandum and they are $36.2 billion today, to the dollar. What changed is the piece bolted on next to them: the spare engines and the GE Aerospace maintenance contract went from about $13.7 billion over 20 years to $8.6 billion over 15, on the same 28 aircraft. Korean Air's own release now describes last year's plan as a $44.8 billion plan. Last year's release said $50 billion.

That is what an aircraft order headline is: a jet figure that never gets renegotiated in public, sitting next to a services contract where the terms actually get reworked. Read the second line. It is the only one that changed.
