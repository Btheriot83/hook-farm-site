When a forty-ton semi loses its brakes at 90 mph down a mountain, it turns into an unstoppable iron giant—until the driver aims for an emergency runaway ramp, where deep gravel instantly swallows the tires and drags the rig to a brutal stop, sinking it so heavy crews must dig it out. Would you have the nerve to steer into a wall of gravel at top speed to live, or try to ride it out down the road?
