CHICAGO LYFT DRIVER TRAUMATIZED AFTER PASSENGER'S IN-APP EMERGENCY ALERT GOES UNANSWERED

A Chicago-area Lyft driver, Jaimee Pinkney, was left terrified after a male passenger removed his pants and began masturbating in the backseat shortly after being picked up from Ingalls Memorial Hospital. Pinkney repeatedly activated the in-app emergency alert, but police did not arrive during the ride, prompting authorities to later advise drivers to dial 911 directly in future emergencies. Police eventually located and arrested the suspect after Pinkney identified him at a nearby hospital.

Chicago Police Department year-end data recorded 1,764 reported criminal sexual assaults last year, with the city's arrest rate for these offenses dropping to 2%.
