TESLA just launched 45 Cybercab ROBOTAXIS in Austin, Texas.

These cars don’t have steering wheels or pedals. They were built from day one to drive themselves and carry passengers without anyone sitting behind the wheel.

Taxi drivers thought 50–80% platform commissions were bad.

Now imagine getting 0%.

Because a robotaxi doesn’t need a salary, tips, breaks, sleep or a share of every ride. It can keep working for most of the day and only stop to charge, clean or get serviced.

That changes the economics completely.

A human driver has to make enough money from each ride to justify being there. Cybercab only has to earn more over its lifetime than it costs to buy, charge and maintain.

The more hours it spends on the road, the cheaper every mile becomes.

And if fleets like this scale, taxi drivers won’t be arguing with Uber or Lyft about commissions anymore.

They’ll be competing with a car that works for $0 an hour.
