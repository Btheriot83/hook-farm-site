Actually difficult to think of a situation where digital forensics wouldn't be involved in any police inquiry. Pretty much everything we use today has some digital aspect to it whether it's your car with all the computers on board recording how you're driving and what's happening just before a crash. Your mobile phone and your tablet recording everything you share about your lifestyle, your interests, your hobbies, the photographs, what you're eating and so on. Um even your desktop and laptop computers that you're using to write essay or send letters, send email and so on. Everyone has a digital life and aspects of that digital life will be relevant to an inquiry at some stage.
