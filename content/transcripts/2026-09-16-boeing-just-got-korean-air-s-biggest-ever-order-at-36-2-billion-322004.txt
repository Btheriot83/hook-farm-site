Boeing Just Got Korean Air's Biggest Ever Order at $36.2 Billion? 👇
⠀
Boeing ( $BA ) and Korean Air announced on Wednesday (2026-09-16) that Korean Air has finalized an order for 103 Boeing aircraft at a list price of $36.2 billion.
⠀
The package covers wide-body jets, freighters, and narrow-body aircraft, and it completes a deal that was first announced in 2025.
⠀
The order breaks down to 20 777-9s, 25 787-10s, 50 737-10s, and 8 777-8 Freighters.
⠀
Boeing says this deal came out of bilateral trade discussions.
⠀
It's the largest order in Korean Air's history and was first made public during South Korean President Lee Jae-myung's visit to Washington last year.

▋ IBA Values It at $12.6B, but the Full Package Hits $44.8B
⠀
Aviation advisory and intelligence firm IBA estimates these aircraft are worth around $12.6 billion at current market prices after discounts, well below the $36.2 billion list price.
⠀
Korean Air says the overall procurement package is worth $44.8 billion.
⠀
That includes a separate $8.6 billion deal with GE Aerospace and CFM International for 21 spare engines, plus a 15-year engine maintenance agreement covering 28 aircraft.

▋ About 80% of the Jets Replace Existing Fleet
⠀
Korean Air says this investment will support long-term fleet expansion following its integration of Asiana Airlines, and it'll help improve fuel efficiency as the airline transitions to newer models.
⠀
CEO Cho Won-tae has said the newer aircraft will help Korean Air expand to more destinations in the U.S. and Latin America.
⠀
Around 80% of the aircraft will replace planes already in the fleet.

▋ Right Now Only the 787 Has Operating Certification
⠀
Of the models in this order, only the 787 currently holds commercial operating certification.
⠀
Boeing is still developing the 777-8 Freighter, which is a real limitation on delivery timelines for this deal.
