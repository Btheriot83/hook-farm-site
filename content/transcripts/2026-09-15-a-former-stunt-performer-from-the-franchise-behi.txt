A former stunt performer from the franchise behind Power Rangers reportedly went from playing heroes to breaking into homes.

Yasutomo Ihara, who worked on Japan’s Super Sentai series, allegedly used his climbing and acrobatic skills to burglarize homes after a knee injury ended his stunt career. His unusual climbing abilities earned him the nickname “Spider Man Thief.”

Authorities reportedly linked him to at least 43 burglaries across Japan between 2013 and 2014, involving roughly 8.2 million yen in stolen property. He was reportedly sentenced to five years in prison.
